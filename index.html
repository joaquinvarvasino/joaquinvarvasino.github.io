About

Academic profile

Joaquin Varvasino is a PhD Student in Economics at Universitat Pompeu Fabra. He works on questions at the intersection of education, labor markets, and inequality, using empirical methods to study how institutions and policy shape long-run outcomes.

His current agenda includes work on the labor-market consequences of tuition-free higher education, peer effects and grade retention in Chile, and new projects in political economy and educational quality.

Research

Working paper

Working Paper with Guillermo Alves

Lasting Scars: Unemployment, Inequality, and Structural Transformation in Latin America

This paper studies the long-run effects of adverse labor-market conditions at labor-market entry in Latin America, showing how early-career unemployment can deepen inequality and shape occupational trajectories, especially for individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds.
